Privacy statement

What data this website processes, why, and what your rights are.

Who is responsible?

Advocatenkantoor de Baarsjes, Hoofdweg 180-hs, 1057 DD Amsterdam, is the controller for personal data processed through this website. Questions about privacy can be sent to info@baarsjes-advocaten.nl or asked by phone on 020 – 618 10 46.

This statement covers the website. Data we receive once you become a client is processed as part of the engagement and under our professional duty of confidentiality as lawyers.

The contact form

When you fill in the contact form, we receive your name, email address, phone number (if given), the subject and your message. We use this only to answer your question and to schedule an introductory meeting if you want one. The legal basis is your request for contact (steps prior to entering into a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

The form is handled technically by our website administrator Perceptum (trading as Bollenstreek Digitaal), which forwards your message to us by email. Perceptum processes the data only on our behalf.

Please do not send confidential documents or sensitive details about your case through the form. We prefer to discuss those in the introductory meeting.

If you do not become a client, we delete your message no later than one year after our last contact.

Visitor statistics

We want to know how the website is used, for example which pages are viewed. For this we use LiveView, a statistics service from Perceptum. LiveView sets no cookies, but it does store a random visitor number in your browser's storage. That number stays there until you remove it. Per visit we record: the pages viewed, the website you came from, your screen size, your browser's language and time zone, how far you scroll and how long you stay on a page. Your IP address is made untraceable immediately and is not stored. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a well-functioning website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We keep this data no longer than needed for the statistics. The statistics are not shared with others and are not used to track you on other websites.

You can remove the visitor number at any time by clearing the site data in your browser.

Google Maps and fonts

The map on the home and contact pages only loads when you click “Show map”. At that moment Google receives your IP address, among other data. The fonts on this website are served from our own server; no connection to Google is made for them.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., United States). As with any website, the host receives technical data such as your IP address in order to serve the page. GitHub participates in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to have its processing restricted, to data portability, and to object to its processing. Send an email to info@baarsjes-advocaten.nl. We respond within one month.

If you disagree with how we handle your data, you can lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
