Not satisfied?

Do you disagree with our work? Or with a bill? Tell us. We would rather solve it together with you.

Step 1
Call or email us

Often it is a misunderstanding. A conversation is the quickest way to clear it up.

020 – 618 10 46
Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:30

Or email info@baarsjes-advocaten.nl.

Tell us you have a question about your bill or about our work. We usually call you back the same working day.

Step 2
Make a complaint to our office. It is free.

Can you not resolve it with your lawyer? Then make a complaint.

  • Send an email or letter. Include: your name, the name of your lawyer, what you think went wrong and what you would like.
  • Do you find writing difficult? Call us. We will write down your complaint for you and send it to you to check.
  • Do this within 3 months of noticing the problem.

The other lawyer at our office looks at your complaint. He talks to you and to your own lawyer. Within 4 weeks you receive a written answer.

Step 3
Still not satisfied? Go to the Disputes Committee.

The Geschillencommissie Advocatuur (Disputes Committee for the Legal Profession) is independent. It decides on disagreements about a bill or about the quality of our work. Its decision is binding for you and for us.

  • You can only go there after step 2.
  • You pay a small complaint fee. The amount is on the committee’s website.
  • Have you not yet paid a bill? Then you deposit that amount with the committee. It holds the money until the decision.
  • Do this within 12 months of our answer.

degeschillencommissie.nl (in Dutch) · phone +31 70 310 53 10

Is your question about your own contribution?

Do you have subsidised legal aid (a toevoeging)? Then the Raad voor Rechtsbijstand (Legal Aid Board) sets the amount of your own contribution. We do not. You do pay the contribution to us.

Do you disagree with the amount? Then you can object to the Raad voor Rechtsbijstand. Do this within 6 weeks of the date on their letter. We are happy to help you with that.
