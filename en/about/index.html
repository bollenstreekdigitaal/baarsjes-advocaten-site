About us

For more than 25 years, the law firm of the neighbourhood in Amsterdam West.

Our firm

Baarsjes Law Firm was founded in 1997 and has been based on the Hoofdweg in Amsterdam West ever since. It is deliberately a small firm — no expensive overheads, no large organisation between you and your lawyer. The lines are direct.

The philosophy is simple: good legal help should be accessible, whatever your situation. Whether you are walking into a lawyer's office for the first time or have been litigating for years — you get the same attention and the same quality.

Mr. A.J. (Bram) van Ommeren — Lawyer & partner

Bram van Ommeren studied law in Amsterdam and has been practising as a lawyer since 1994. He has been a partner at Baarsjes Law Firm since 1997. He is known for his direct approach: getting to the heart of the matter quickly and working efficiently, so you do not pay for unnecessary hours.

He is registered in the following practice areas: family law, criminal defence, tenancy law and general practice. For questions or to make an appointment, you can reach him directly at info@baarsjes-advocaten.nl.

  • Lawyer since 1994
  • Registrations: family law, criminal defence, tenancy law, general practice
  • Languages: Dutch, English, French and German

Mr. C.R. (Clemens) Hettema — Lawyer & partner

Mr. Hettema has been a lawyer in Amsterdam since 1997. His education: gymnasium (Dutch grammar school), law at the University of Amsterdam and the Dutch bar traineeship. He also takes part in continuing legal education every year, with a focus on personal and family law.

In 1993 he co-founded Hettema & van Bambost Juristen, a firm for legal advice and debt collection. In 1997 he left to co-found Baarsjes Law Firm, where he is now a partner.

Mr. Hettema is a committed lawyer. He first tries to settle matters by mutual agreement. If that does not work, the case can go to court. He has extensive litigation experience across many areas of law.

He is listed in the Netherlands Bar's register of practice areas for personal and family law, tenancy law, criminal law and general practice. This requires him to earn training points every year. He speaks Dutch, English, German and French.

You can reach Mr. Hettema at clemens@advocatenkantoordebaarsjes.com. For urgent matters, call him directly on (Mon–Fri).

  • Lawyer since 1997
  • Co-founder and partner of Baarsjes Law Firm
  • Registrations: personal and family law, tenancy law, criminal law, general practice
  • Languages: Dutch, English, German and French
