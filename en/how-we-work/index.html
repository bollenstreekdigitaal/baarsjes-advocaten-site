How we work

No surprises. We tell you upfront what to expect and which costs we foresee.

1

Introductory meeting

The first conversation lasts 30 minutes and is free. You tell us your story, we listen. Together we look at what is going on and what you can expect.

  • You tell us your story, we listen
  • An initial assessment of your case
  • Explanation of options and costs
  • Free and without obligation — you decide whether to go further
2

Setting the strategy

If you decide to continue, we set the approach together. What is the goal? Which steps are needed? What will it cost? You get clear answers, in plain language.

  • Together we set the approach
  • Clear advice in plain language, no jargon
  • Clarity on costs upfront
  • You know exactly where you stand
3

Getting to work

Once you agree, we get to work for you. You are kept informed at every step. No surprises afterwards.

  • We get to work for you
  • You are kept informed at every step
  • As a rule, letters and documents go to you in draft first
  • We are available for questions

What you can expect from us

Beyond the three steps, we hold ourselves to a number of concrete commitments.

  • Phone availability — as a rule we call you back the same working day
  • Quick correspondence — post is handled as soon as possible
  • Transparency — always clear on progress and costs
  • Complaints procedure — we have an office complaints procedure; complaints can be sent to info@baarsjes-advocaten.nl
